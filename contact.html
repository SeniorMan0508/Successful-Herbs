Common Questions & Guidance

How do I take the herbal mixtures?

Each bottle comes with detailed dosage guidelines. Typically, our liquid tonics are taken in small tot glasses (30ml - 50ml) morning before breakfast and evening after meals. Exact instructions are provided with your order.

How long does delivery take across Nigeria?

Orders within Abeokuta are delivered same-day. Orders to Lagos and nearby Ogun towns arrive within 24 hours. Deliveries to other Nigerian states (Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan, etc.) take between 1 to 3 business days via verified transit couriers.

Are there synthetic preservatives in Successful Herbs?

No. All our formulas rely on natural bio-preservatives (indigenous lime, ginger, and traditional extraction methods) with zero artificial chemical preservatives or synthetic additives.
